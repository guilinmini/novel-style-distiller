# Foreshadowing, promises, and payoff

Use this module when opening, advancing, delaying, reframing, or paying off plot threads and reader promises.

## Thread lifecycle

Track every consequential thread as:

```text
setup → reinforcement/complication → reframe or narrowing
→ readiness → payoff → aftermath
```

Not every thread needs every stage, but every setup that reads as a promise needs a decision: pay it, transform it, explicitly abandon it with consequence, or remove the setup during revision.

## Setup strength

A setup becomes memorable through one or more of:

- immediate practical consequence;
- emotional attachment;
- repeated but varied appearance;
- contradiction or unexplained absence;
- connection to a character goal;
- specific sensory or object state.

Stronger setup creates a stronger payoff obligation. Avoid emphasizing decorative details in the same way as critical clues.

## Fair misdirection

Misdirection should offer a plausible interpretation of true evidence. It should not require false narration, hidden POV knowledge, or facts invented at reveal time.

Record separately:

- author truth;
- intended reader hypothesis;
- evidence supporting both surface and deeper interpretations;
- reveal event that makes the deeper interpretation actionable.

## Payoff quality

A payoff is proportionate when it:

- answers the promised kind of question;
- follows from planted information and character action;
- changes current options or relationships;
- carries emotional or practical consequence;
- may create a new question without evading the old one.

Recognition alone is not always payoff; readers often need to see what the answer costs or enables.

## Thread aging

Use `state/PLOT_THREADS.md` to record last-touched chapter and due window. During chapter preparation:

1. identify overdue high-priority promises;
2. distinguish deliberate long delay from accidental neglect;
3. advance only threads that belong in the current causal cluster;
4. avoid touching every thread superficially;
5. include aftermath after major payoff.
