# Natural prose diagnostics

Use this module during chapter review when prose feels generated, generic, over-smoothed, repetitive, or disconnected from character-specific causality. “Remove AI flavor” is not an actionable edit until a concrete symptom is identified.

## Common synthetic symptoms

| Symptom | Why it feels synthetic | Focused repair |
|---|---|---|
| Uniform paragraph and sentence cadence | no change in thought or scene pressure affects rhythm | vary through scene function within the style contract |
| Repeated three-part parallelism | rhetorical shape appears without character need | keep only the strongest concrete distinction |
| Emotion is named, restated, then explained | narration distrusts action and subtext | retain the consequence-bearing signal |
| Generic sensory detail | interchangeable light, wind, silence, heartbeat | choose detail the POV would notice for a current reason |
| Decorative metaphor chains | images do not alter interpretation | keep one image tied to project-specific perception |
| Everyone speaks complete thematic sentences | speakers share one explanatory voice | restore objectives, evasion, vocabulary, status, and interruption |
| Transition over-signaling | every inference is connected and summarized | let spatial, causal, or conversational continuity carry obvious links |
| Paragraph-end moral or verdict | scenes repeatedly close themselves twice | end on changed action, image, implication, or pressure |
| False precision | invented quantities or technical claims imply unsupported authority | use verified canon or honest uncertainty |
| Recap before every action | the text repeatedly reopens settled context | load memory internally; expose only what the present choice needs |
| Mechanical hook ending | every chapter adds a knock, message, shadow, or “suddenly” | select a hook caused by the chapter's actual turn |
| Abstract escalation | narration says stakes rose but options remain the same | change a deadline, resource, relationship, rule, or available choice |

## Revision sequence

1. Mark the exact repeated or generic pattern; do not globally rewrite by intuition.
2. Recover the POV's goal, knowledge, relationship, and immediate physical situation.
3. Replace abstraction with a choice, consequence, project-specific observation, or omitted implication.
4. Preserve the locked style contract. Naturalness is not permission to flatten a deliberately formal or lyrical voice.
5. Read adjacent paragraphs and recent chapter endings for recurrence.
6. Recheck facts, POV, and hook obligations after the prose edit.

## Character-specificity test

For an important paragraph or dialogue turn, ask:

- Could another active character say or notice this unchanged?
- Does the detail arise from this person's knowledge, desire, occupation, status, or history?
- Does the sentence change if the relationship target changes?
- Is the rhythm responding to present pressure or merely repeating the model's favorite cadence?

## Batch drift checks

Every checkpoint group should compare recent chapters for:

- repeated opening construction;
- repeated exit-hook family;
- repeated metaphor domains and emotional gestures;
- identical dialogue rhythm across characters;
- growing explanation density;
- shrinking causal specificity;
- style-contract invariants that have quietly disappeared.

Record symptoms and repairs in `state/SERIAL_RHYTHM.md` and the relevant chapter record. Do not store vague labels such as “AI score.”
