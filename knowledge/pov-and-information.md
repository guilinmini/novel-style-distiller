# POV and information control

Use this module for focalization, character knowledge, mystery fairness, reveal timing, unreliable narration, or jump-head diagnosis.

## Three knowledge layers

Keep separate:

1. **Author truth**: what is actually true in the project.
2. **Character knowledge**: what each character knows, believes, suspects, or misunderstands.
3. **Reader knowledge**: what the text has confirmed, suggested, concealed, or misdirected.

Record changes in `state/KNOWLEDGE_LEDGER.md`. A character cannot act on author truth without a recorded acquisition path.

## POV contract

For each scene define:

- grammatical person and tense;
- focalizer;
- allowed interior access;
- baseline narrative distance;
- what the narrator can name or interpret;
- how and where POV may switch.

Sensory detail, comparisons, judgments, and noticed objects should be plausible for the focalizer unless an established narrator supplies them.

## Reveal design

A useful reveal changes at least one of:

- the meaning of prior facts;
- the range of available actions;
- trust between characters;
- the expected cost or objective;
- the reader's prediction of causality.

Before a major reveal, plant fair support in varied forms: action consequence, absence, contradiction, object state, testimony, or behavior. Do not repeat the same clue until it becomes an announcement.

## Withholding without cheating

Withhold because:

- the focalizer does not know;
- the focalizer misinterprets;
- the scene goal directs attention elsewhere;
- the narrator has an established reason or limitation;
- the information has not yet become actionable.

Do not make a POV character unnaturally avoid thinking a fact solely to deceive the reader.

## Multi-POV management

Give each POV a distinct access function, not just a different location. Track:

- what this POV can reveal that others cannot;
- which story pressure they own;
- handoff timing and unresolved question;
- how much reader advantage or disadvantage the switch creates.

Switch only at established boundaries unless the project explicitly uses controlled omniscience.

## Information audit

For every consequential action, trace:

```text
fact source → acquisition event → character interpretation
→ decision → reader exposure → later consequence
```

A missing link is a continuity defect, even if the action would be convenient for the plot.
